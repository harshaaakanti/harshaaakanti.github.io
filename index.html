Profile

Defender with an attacker's background

Three and a half years inside a global enterprise SOC at a cloud-security company, preceded by three years hunting web vulnerabilities on public and private bug bounty programs.

That mix shapes how I work. I read alerts the way an intruder would read a target, and I automate the parts of the job that should never need a human: enrichment, reputation lookups, asset context, ticket drafting. Analysts then spend their time on the decisions that matter. I own incidents from the first alert to the final forensic report, and I lead the training for each year's SOC intern cohort.

Field notes

Positions I argue for

AI will not replace L1 analysts. Analysts who use it will outpace those who don't.

Automation should strip out the boilerplate of stitching telemetry and drafting tickets, so people spend their energy on real hunting and avoid burnout.

MFA protects the login. Attackers now skip the login.

Info-stealers lift session cookies and tokens. The answer is short sessions, token binding, session-reuse detection and revoking sessions during response.
